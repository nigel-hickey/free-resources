# Sample Recipe: Mushroom Cream Sauce with Beer Bratwurst over Spaetzle

The inventory and shopping list are the two files to start with. This one is a bonus, straight out of my own recipe collection, because it's a good example of what the system can actually do once it's running.

This recipe didn't exist anywhere before this cook. I had beer bratwurst, mushrooms, a container of Knorr Rahm sauce base, and a bag of spaetzle, and no plan. I told the AI what I had and asked it to build something out of it instead of following a fix packet. What came back is below, and it's stayed in my recipe file ever since.

**Servings:** 2

**Ingredients**
- Deutsche Küche Uncured Beer Bratwurst (Aldi), 3 to 4 fully cooked links
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced or chopped small
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp Knorr Rahm sauce powder
- 1 and 1/4 cups cold water
- 1/4 to 1/2 cup heavy cream, to taste
- 1 tbsp butter
- Oil for the pan
- Dried parsley, a pinch, for finishing
- Bechtle Farmer Style Spaetzle, about half a 500g bag for two
- Salt for the pasta water

**Steps**
1. Get salted water boiling for the spaetzle.
2. Heat a large pan over medium high with a drizzle of oil. Brown the whole brats on two or three sides, about 3 to 4 minutes. They're already fully cooked, so this is for crust only.
3. In the last minute, add the garlic to the pan alongside the brats and let it soften in the drippings. Watch it closely. If it starts moving too fast, pull the brats and garlic together onto a plate and hold them.
4. Slice the brats into thick coins. Return the cut faces to the pan for a minute or two to brown, then set aside with the garlic.
5. Drop the heat to medium, add the butter, and once melted add the mushrooms. Let them sit undisturbed for 2 minutes to brown instead of steam, then toss until golden.
6. Drop the spaetzle into the boiling water.
7. Return the brat coins and garlic to the pan with the mushrooms and stir together.
8. Pour in the cold water and whisk in the Rahm powder. Bring to a gentle simmer and let it thicken, about 2 to 3 minutes, stirring.
9. Turn the heat to low, let the pan settle for a moment, then stir in the cream. Taste before adding any salt, the Rahm powder and drippings usually carry plenty already.
10. Drain the spaetzle, plate it, spoon the sauce and brats over the top, and finish with a pinch of parsley.

**Notes**
- Fully cooked sausage only needs a sear for flavor, not a full cook.
- Browning the mushrooms undisturbed first keeps them from steaming into a gray, watery mess.
- Whisking the Rahm powder into cold water before it hits the heat keeps it from clumping.
- Taste before you salt anything. A packet sauce base and meat drippings are usually already seasoned.
